# Pak Hei's Argyle Secondary Schedule & Bell Tracker (2026–2027)

Interactive, real-time class schedule, bell tracker, holiday reminder, and homework planner for **Pak Hei Leung** (Grade 9, Homeroom `H09-06`) at **École Secondaire Argyle Secondary**, North Vancouver, BC.

## Features
- **Auto-Updating 2-Week Block Rotation**: Follows Argyle's Day 1 / Day 2 and Week 1 / Week 2 rotation (`1234`, `5678`, `2314`, `6758`, `3124`, `7568`).
- **Automated Bell Schedules**: Automatically detects Regular (9:10 AM – 3:00 PM), Late Start Wednesday (9:48 AM – 3:00 PM), and Early Dismissal (1:50 PM) days.
- **Same-Day & Next-Day Reminders**: Pro-D days, statutory holidays, RCE days, and school events with explicit `(no school)` and `(yes school)` badges and times.
- **Homework Planner**: Attach notes and due dates to specific classes with automatic reminder banners.
- **Dark & Light Mode**: High-contrast theme toggle with persistence.
- **Mobile PWA Support**: Add to iPhone or Android Home Screen for full-screen app access.
